## 6. Key business insights (from the data)

1. Revenue grew **+22.1% (CAGR 6.9%)** from 2021 to 2024, but new-customer acquisition fell **from 1,312 to 66** — growth now depends on retention.

3. **30%+ discounts lose money (−8.2% margin, −$305K)** with no volume lift (r = 0.004); no-discount lines earn 32.1% margin vs 25.3% for discounted lines.

5. **December demand is 2.4x July** — peak-season planning matters.
6. **123 at-risk/Can't-Lose customers hold $4.0M** of recoverable revenue; 536 Lost customers hold $3.0M.

## 7. Interview explanation — STAR / structured approach

**Situation:** A multi-region retail company (2021–2024) knew *what* it sold but not *which* customers, products, discounts and regions to invest in. I was asked to turn transaction data into actionable decisions.

**Task:** Deliver a complete analysis: clean the data, answer 12 business questions, prove the discount question statistically, segment customers, and present recommendations to management in an executive dashboard and report.

**Action:**
- Audited and cleaned 117K raw records (missing values, duplicates, invalid dates, price typos, inconsistent casing, non-completed orders) into a validated 108K-line dataset with 10+ engineered features (age groups, discount bands, date parts).
- Ran EDA (time trends, seasonality, Pareto, geography, discounts) and formal tests: Welch t-tests (discount margin impact; tier AOV), ANOVA (margin by category; spend by segment), Pearson correlations (discount vs quantity/profit; age vs spend) — each with H0/H1, test statistic, p-value and decision.
- Built an RFM segmentation (Recency/Frequency/Monetary, quintile scores) into Champions, Loyal, At-Risk, Can't-Lose, Lost and others.
- Created a normalised SQLite database with 21 business/advanced queries (rankings, running totals, MoM growth, RFM-in-SQL, Pareto) and a 4-page Power BI dashboard (KPIs, slicers, Region→State→City drill-down).

**Result:**
- Quantified $68.9M revenue / $16.4M profit / 23.8% margin / 33,209 orders / 1,648 customers / $2,074 AOV.
- Proved discounts above ~15% destroy profit without volume growth (−8.2% margin at 30%+) — supporting a discount-cap policy.
- Found 33% of customers = 80% of revenue and $4.0M of revenue sitting with at-risk customers, prioritising a retention and win-back plan.
- Delivered 8 prioritised management recommendations (discount policy, Champion retention, Central-region expansion, high-margin cross-sell, seasonal calendar, B2B programme).
